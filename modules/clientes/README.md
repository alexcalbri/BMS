# Clientes Module

Status: locked base module scaffold.

Clientes exposes the shared BMS Customer/Prospect identity to the UI and
other modules. Because every company needs the customer surface, new tenant
databases install this module automatically and seed it as enabled in
`tenant_modules`.

The customer schema is owned by this module and lives in:

```text
modules/clientes/migrations/V001__create_customers.sql
```

Implemented baseline:

- `GET /modules/clientes/customers` lists customers for users with
  `clientes.view`, ordered by name. Without parameters it answers the first page (up to 200) as before; when
  there are more, the answer carries `nextCursor`, to be sent back as `after`. `q` searches by the start of the
  name, email or phone, or the exact customer code; `limit` (1-200) sets the page size. The indexes of V011
  (`text_pattern_ops`, no `pg_trgm`: company roles are not superusers) serve the prefix search and the cursor.
  `PUT /customers/{id}` answers 409 `CUSTOMER_DUPLICATE_EMAIL` when the new email belongs to a customer of a
  linked company (also through the agent tool).
- `POST /modules/clientes/customers` creates customers for users with
  `clientes.create`.
- Creating a customer requires only Nombre; Correo and Telefono are optional.
  Since migration V005 the database accepts an empty email or phone (stored as
  `''`, never NULL), so modules such as CRM can register a person known only
  by name and one contact channel.
- Every customer has a `customer_code` (migration V004): 8 random characters
  without look-alikes (no 0/O, 1/I), unique per company and generated by the
  database on insert, including for customers that existed before. It is
  read-only and shown in the customer list; modules such as Hotspot use it to
  identify the customer (e.g. WiFi access by code).
- Extra Customer/Core fields created from the Clientes administration surface
  are backed by `customer_field_definitions` and stored in
  `customers.flexible_attributes`.
- Module-specific fields must be stored by the owning module, not in the shared
  Clientes field table, unless they are intentionally promoted to the shared
  customer record.

## AI agent tools

With the `agentes` module, agents can use (`ClientesMcpTools.kt`), with the same permissions and
validations as the routes:

| Tool | Permission | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `clientes_search_customers`, `clientes_get_customer`, `clientes_list_customer_fields` | `clientes.view` | Search customers, read one by id or code, list the custom fields. |
| `clientes_create_customer` | `clientes.create` | Creates a customer (name required, no duplicated email). |
| `clientes_update_customer` | `clientes.edit` | Changes only the data sent. |
| `clientes_archive_customer` | `clientes.delete` | Archives a customer, like `DELETE /customers/{id}`. |
| `clientes_get_customer_history` | `clientes.view` | The customer's history, like `GET /customers/{id}/history`. |
| `clientes_create_customer_field` | `clientes.fields.manage` | Adds a custom field, like `POST /fields`. |
| `clientes_delete_customer_field` | `clientes.fields.manage` | Hides a custom field (values are kept), like `DELETE /fields/{id}`. |
| `clientes_restore_customer` | `clientes.delete` | Restores an archived customer, like `POST /customers/{id}/restore`. |
| `clientes_get_company_links`, `clientes_list_merge_reviews` | `clientes.share` + business owner | Linked and linkable companies with the copy progress; merges done or to review. |
| `clientes_link_company`, `clientes_unlink_company`, `clientes_separate_merge`, `clientes_merge_manually` | `clientes.share` + business owner | Link or unlink a company; undo a merge; merge by hand two customers with the same email and different name (or keep them apart with `clientes_separate_merge`). The handler checks business owner on top of the permission. |

`clientes_search_customers` searches by the start of name, email or phone or the exact code and pages with
`nextCursor` / `after`; `clientes_list_merge_reviews` returns `reasonCode` (`name_differs`, `accounts`, `code`,
`incomplete`, `copy`, `edit`) next to `reason`.

Creating, editing, archiving, restoring and field changes made by an agent are logged like the routes
(author "name (agente X)"); a new customer is copied to the linked companies at once.

## Customer profile (ficha) and sharing (V007)

The app's *Ficha* button opens the customer's profile, drawn from the native screens of
`/modules/clientes/ui/customer?id=...&tab=...` (server-driven: it changes without updating the app):

- **One record for the linked companies.** A customer belongs to the company where it was
  created (`origin_company_code` is `''` there). The business owner links companies of their owner group
  in "Empresas vinculadas" (`GET/POST /modules/clientes/ui/links`, business owner only, checked on the
  server; Core table `company_links`, purpose `clientes`). Linking shares ALL customers: each company
  copies its own to the others in the background (`customer_link_jobs`, job `link-sync`), copies keep the
  id and customer code, and every edit, archive or restore reaches every copy. Customers with the same
  email AND the same name are merged automatically (the oldest stays); the same email with a different name is
  never merged alone: it goes to "Por revisar" with the reason "Mismo correo, nombre distinto" (`reason_code`
  `name_differs`), where the owner chooses "Fusionar" or "Dejar separados". Other pairs that could not be merged
  go there too, with their reason, and can be retried or separated (`customer_merges`). Unlinking hides the copies and keeps history.
  Only the origin archives or restores a customer. Lists, searches and agents only see the company's
  own customers and the ones shared with it. `clientes.share` is kept only to enable the agent tools.
- **Notas del cliente** (`customer_notes`, in the origin company's database): tastes and preferences
  of frequent customers and general information, seen in every company where the customer is and
  in its CRM leads (`ServerModule.customerNotesReader`, read with `access.customerNotes`).
  Users with `clientes.edit` add notes; the author or the business owner deletes them.
- **One tab per company** with what the customer bought or hired there: the events modules write
  (`customer_history`, see "Writing events in the customer's history" in `modules/README.md`; the
  CRM writes "Venta convertida en {empresa}" with a link to the lead), entries added by hand
  (`clientes.edit`, in the current company), the changes of the record and what other modules
  report. The business owner sees the tab of every company where the customer is, read with that
  company's own permissions (business owner there, no user id or grants of the current company); other users
  only the tab of the company they are part of, even if they ask for another `tab`. A "Resumen" at the top
  lists, per visible company, the modules with something in the customer's history, with count and last date.
  The `customer_history` rows of a module show only while the module is on and the user may see its events
  (`ModuleAccessService.customerEventsVisible`).

Other modules that change a customer's data (the CRM editing a lead's name, email or phone, filling
a name from WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram, or importing custom fields) call
`access.customerChanged(tenant, customerId)` once the change is committed, and Clientes copies it at
once to every company the customer is shared with.

## Customer history

`GET /modules/clientes/customers/{id}/history` (`clientes.view`) returns what happened with a
customer, newest first: who created, edited (field by field, before and after) or archived it in
Clientes (`customer_activity`) and what was written in this company's history (`customer_history`),
plus what every installed module reports through its `CustomerTimeline` (CRM reservations, hotspot
WiFi connections...). Each
module's entries appear only when it is enabled and the user holds its permission. Clientes knows no
module: see "Customer history" in `modules/README.md`.

## Activity log

`customer_activity` records who did what: customer created, edited (field by field, custom fields included),
archived and restored (`POST /customers/{id}/restore`, `clientes.delete`, origin company only), notes deleted
(with their text), manual history entries deleted (the profile lists them; only module `clientes`, kind
`manual`), and merged/separated customers. Events of the company, without a customer (`customer_id` NULL):
custom field created or deleted (`DELETE /fields/{id}` hides it, keeps the values), company linked or
unlinked. They appear in "Registro de Clientes" (`GET /modules/clientes/ui/activity`, business owner or
`clientes.fields.manage`); the customer's own events appear in its profile.

## Linking notes

- `clientes.share` only enables the agent tools for linking and merging (`clientes_get_company_links`,
  `clientes_link_company`, `clientes_unlink_company`, `clientes_list_merge_reviews`, `clientes_separate_merge`);
  each of them still requires the business owner, checked in the handler before anything else.
- Customers created by other modules (CRM, hotspot...) are copied to the linked companies by the `link-sync`
  job, within the next minute. Customers created in Clientes (route or agent) are copied at once.
- Automatic merge needs the same email and the same name: the oldest customer stays, the other is archived and its
  notes and records move to it. With the same email and another name nothing is merged alone (this replaces the
  earlier policy of merging by email only). "Fusionar" (`clientes_merge_manually`) merges them by the owner's decision,
  moving references and history without completing fields; "Dejar separados" / "Separar" keeps them apart for good,
  and "Separar" also undoes a merge already done. Fields completed in the kept customer by an earlier merge are not undone.
- Emails: a hidden copy (company no longer linked) does not block an email (`CustomerSql.Visible`).
- The customer's profile reads company names once per request and each company's history once (summary and open tab share it).
- Separating is not a two-phase commit between company databases: each database is changed in its own
  transaction, so a failure in the middle can leave one company done and another pending; retry from "Por revisar".
- Unlinking keeps the merges already done; use "Separar" first if the records must go back.
- Routes do their database work on `Dispatchers.IO`; archive and restore answer 404 for a hidden copy and 409
  for a visible copy (only the origin company archives or restores).

## User accounts (V009, 0.10.0)

A user account of the company (`application_users`) can be linked to a customer card (`customer_accounts`,
`account_kind = 'user'`): the card is the person's record, so a user who also buys services has one record.
It is optional and lives in the company of the account; the card must be visible there.

- **Anti-absorption rule.** An account is only linked to the card whose email equals the account's email
  (case ignored, never empty). A card with another email or without one is never used. If no visible card has
  that email, the agent tool creates one with the account's name and email (activity `created`, then shared with
  the linked companies). An account without email cannot be linked.
- **Uniqueness.** One account, one card; one user account per card (clear error otherwise).
- **No permissions.** The link grants nothing and no authorization check reads it.
- **Who links.** The business owner or whoever holds `clientes.accounts.link`; there is no self-service, a user
  cannot link their own account without that permission.
- **Profile.** Roles line ("Cliente" always, "Usuario" when linked; the account's name and email only for who
  may link) and a "Cuenta de usuario" section with Enlazar (free accounts with the card's email) and Desenlazar.
  Both are ops of `POST /modules/clientes/ui/customer` (`linkuser`, `unlinkuser`) and are logged as
  `user_linked` / `user_unlinked`. Employee, owner and agent roles are not derived (Clientes knows no module).
- **Merge and Separar.** `customer_id` is a foreign key, so the link moves with the card when duplicates are
  merged or separated, like any other record; if both cards of a merge have a linked account in the same company the merge goes to "Por revisar": unlink one and press Reintentar. The account's email is not verified: confirm the account belongs to that person (the profile warns when the account's email no longer matches the card).
- **Agent tools** (permission `clientes.accounts.link`): `clientes_link_user_account` (by `userId` or account
  email) and `clientes_unlink_user_account`; `clientes_get_customer` returns `roles` and, only to who may link,
  `userAccount`.
